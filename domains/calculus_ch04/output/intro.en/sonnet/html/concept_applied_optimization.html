Applied Optimization

Definition. Applied optimization is the process of finding the value of an independent variable that maximizes or minimizes a real-valued objective function subject to constraints derived from a physical or economic model. Formally, given a differentiable function $f : D \subseteq \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, a local minimum at $c \in D$ satisfies $f(c) \le f(x)$ for all $x$ in some open interval containing $c$; a global minimum satisfies this inequality over all of $D$. The analogous inequalities with $\ge$ define local and global maxima.

The Closed Interval Method guarantees a global extremum when $f$ is continuous on a closed interval $[a, b]$: evaluate $f$ at every critical point (where $f'(c) = 0$ or $f'(c)$ is undefined) and at the endpoints $a$ and $b$; the largest value is the global maximum and the smallest is the global minimum.

Worked Example. A farmer has 400 m of fencing and wishes to enclose a rectangular field against a straight barn wall, so only three sides require fencing. Let $x$ denote the length of the side perpendicular to the barn and $y$ the side parallel to it. The constraint is $2x + y = 400$, giving $y = 400 - 2x$. The enclosed area is

$$A(x) = x \cdot y = x(400 - 2x) = 400x - 2x^2, \quad x \in [0,\, 200].$$

Setting $A'(x) = 400 - 4x = 0$ yields the critical point $x = 100$ m. Evaluating: $A(0) = 0$, $A(200) = 0$, and $A(100) = 20{,}000\ \text{m}^2$. The maximum enclosed area is $\boxed{20{,}000\ \text{m}^2}$, achieved with dimensions $100\ \text{m} \times 200\ \text{m}$.

Theorem (Second Derivative Test). If $f'(c) = 0$ and $f''(c) > 0$, then $f$ has a local minimum at $c$; if $f''(c) < 0$, then $f$ has a local maximum at $c$. If $f''(c) = 0$, the test is inconclusive. In the example above, $A''(x) = -4 < 0$, confirming $x = 100$ is a maximum.

Lab Cell.

from sympy import symbols, diff, solve, simplify

x = symbols('x', positive=True)
A = 400*x - 2*x**2

critical = solve(diff(A, x), x)
area_max = A.subs(x, critical[0])

print("Critical point:", critical)      # [100]
print("Maximum area:", area_max)        # 20000
print("Second derivative:", diff(A, x, 2))  # -4  (confirms maximum)
