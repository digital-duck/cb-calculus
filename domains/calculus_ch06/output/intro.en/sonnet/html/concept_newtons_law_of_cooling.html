Newtons Law Of Cooling

Definition. Newton's Law of Cooling states that the rate of change of the temperature of an object is proportional to the difference between the object's temperature and the ambient (surrounding) temperature. If $T(t)$ denotes the temperature of the object at time $t$ and $T_a$ denotes the constant ambient temperature, then

$$\frac{dT}{dt} = -k\bigl(T(t) - T_a\bigr), \quad k > 0,$$

where $k$ is a positive constant depending on the physical properties of the object and its environment. The negative sign ensures that the object cools when $T > T_a$ and warms when $T < T_a$.

Worked Example. A cup of coffee at $90\,^\circ\text{C}$ is placed in a room held at $20\,^\circ\text{C}$. After $10$ minutes the coffee has cooled to $70\,^\circ\text{C}$. Find $T(t)$ and determine when the coffee reaches $40\,^\circ\text{C}$.

Let $u(t) = T(t) - 20$. Then $\frac{du}{dt} = -k\,u$, a separable ODE with solution $u(t) = u(0)\,e^{-kt}$. Since $u(0) = 70$,

$$T(t) = 20 + 70\,e^{-kt}.$$

Applying $T(10) = 70$ gives $70\,e^{-10k} = 50$, so

$$k = \frac{1}{10}\ln\!\left(\frac{70}{50}\right) = \frac{\ln 1.4}{10} \approx 0.03365\,\text{min}^{-1}.$$

To find when $T = 40$: $70\,e^{-kt} = 20 \Rightarrow t = \frac{\ln(70/20)}{k} \approx \frac{\ln 3.5}{0.03365} \approx 37.6\,\text{min}$.

Key Theorem. The unique solution to the initial-value problem $\frac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - T_a)$, $T(0) = T_0$, is

$$\boxed{T(t) = T_a + (T_0 - T_a)\,e^{-kt}.}$$

Proof sketch. Separate variables: $\frac{dT}{T - T_a} = -k\,dt$. Integrate both sides to obtain $\ln|T - T_a| = -kt + C$. Exponentiating and applying the initial condition yields the stated formula. Existence and uniqueness follow from the Picard–Lindelöf theorem, since the right-hand side is Lipschitz in $T$. $\blacksquare$

Lab Cell.

import sympy as sp

t, k, T0, Ta = sp.symbols('t k T_0 T_a', positive=True)
T = sp.Function('T')

ode = sp.Eq(T(t).diff(t), -k * (T(t) - Ta))
sol = sp.dsolve(ode, T(t), ics={T(0): T0})
print(sol)

# Numerical example: T0=90, Ta=20, k=ln(1.4)/10
T_num = sol.rhs.subs({T0: 90, Ta: 20, k: sp.Rational(1,10)*sp.ln(sp.Rational(7,5))})
t_val = sp.solve(T_num - 40, t)
print("Reaches 40°C at t =", sp.simplify(t_val[0]))
